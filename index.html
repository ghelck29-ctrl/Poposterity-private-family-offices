Who we are

Posterity is one family’s private office, overseeing its businesses, real estate, investments, and family interests. Rooted in a commitment to stewardship, we support the faith, education, and health institutions we care about and the communities we call home.

What we stand for

Every generation inherits more than what was built before it. We honor those who came before us, rise to what is entrusted, and build a stronger foundation for those who follow.

  • I

    Stewardship

    We are blessed with opportunity and with one another. We care for what we’ve received and leave it stronger for those who follow.

  • II

    Reinvestment

    We build on the foundation of previous generations, reinvesting with discipline and purpose so tomorrow has greater opportunity.

  • III

    Governance

    With opportunity comes responsibility. Our strength is in shared values, clear decisions, and accountability across generations.

  • IV

    Family

    Family is the purpose. We strive to give those who follow a sense of belonging, pride in their heritage, and the opportunity to build a better tomorrow for their own families.

Privately held. Posterity does not seek outside capital or offer services to the public.
